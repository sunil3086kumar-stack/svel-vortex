FAQ

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Entry fee kaise dete hain?

Booking app mein lobby kholo — wahan UPI QR dikhega. GPay, PhonePe ya Paytm se scan karke pay karo, phir screenshot app mein attach kar do. Organizer verify karke slot confirm karega.

Slot kab tak confirm hota hai?

Payment verify hone ke baad turant. Screenshot aur FF UID sahi hone chahiye. Har lobby mein 12 slots hote hain — bhar jaye to "Lobby full" dikhne lagega.

Prize paisa kaise milta hai?

Match ke result ke baad top 3 teams ko prize distribution ke hisaab se paisa diya jata hai — jo lobby card pe likha hota hai (jaise 70 / 55 / 35).

Lobby kis map pe hoti hai?

Har lobby ka map organizer booking app mein set karta hai — Bermuda, Purgatory, Alpine, Kalahari ya Nexterra. Match se pehle group mein bata diya jata hai.

Payment ho gaya par slot nahi mila?

Organizer ko DM karo — number neeche diya hai. Booking code (jaise BK012) aur screenshot bhej do, turant check kar ke slot de diya jayega.
